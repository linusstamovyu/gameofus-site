# GAME OF US — Logo pack

Seven color variations; 4096 × 4096 PNG exports and 1024 × 1024 web exports. Open index.html for a visual chooser. Files ending in transparent use alpha transparency; the three named background variants are opaque compositions.

## Colorways

- **primary-transparent** — Default ivory and navy logo. Use on light or medium backgrounds.
- **inverse-transparent** — Dark lettering with a light silhouette for dark backgrounds.
- **mono-black-transparent** — Black wordmark for light surfaces and one-color reproduction.
- **mono-white-transparent** — White wordmark for dark surfaces and one-color reproduction.
- **white-on-black** — White lettering on an opaque black canvas.
- **black-on-white** — Black lettering on an opaque white canvas.
- **primary-on-blue** — Ivory and navy logo on a cyan blue canvas.

## Usage

Use the primary logo by default. Choose the inverse or white wordmark on dark surfaces and the black wordmark on light surfaces. One-color wordmarks deliberately omit the extrusion so lettering stays readable. Keep the controller cross and four-button detail. Scale proportionally and leave surrounding space; suggested working minimum is 10% of logo width. Check legibility at final size, especially the controller symbols. Do not stretch, crop, add effects, or place on busy backgrounds.

Palette direction: ivory, deep navy and cyan blue, plus neutral black and white. Blue generation target: #009FE3. Generated artwork can contain tonal variation; these are raster assets, not spot-color or vector production masters.

## Research basis

There is no universal mandatory company logo kit. The selection follows common full-color, reverse and monochrome categories:
- MIT Sloan: full-color preferred; reverse for dark backgrounds; black/white single-color for limited reproduction. https://mitsloan.mit.edu/brand-guidelines/logo
- Slack: background usage, one-color use and logo misuse guidance. https://a.slack-edge.com/4d5bb/marketing/img/media-kit/slack_brand_guidelines_september2020.pdf?country=56

The cyan-background composition is a GAME OF US brand application, not a universal kit requirement. No alternate horizontal lockup or icon is invented.

## Production

Built-in imagegen edited the supplied GAME OF US logo. Primary artwork is the previously generated transparent logo. Six new variations were generated from that same reference; small geometry differences may occur between generated colorways. 4K exports are upscaled raster images, not native vector art. Source folder preserves original generation resolutions; background compositions are flattened onto their named solid colors. Web exports are 1024 px. This pack does not change the live website.

Prompt strategy: Preserve exact GAME / OF US lettering, proportions, controller cross in G and four buttons in E; retain the stacked composition. Change only the specified colors/background, removing the extrusion only for plain single-color wordmarks. Remove PRESENTS throughout. Transparent variants request genuine alpha. Full per-variant prompts are in prompts.json.
