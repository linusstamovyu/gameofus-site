What we collect

  • Your contact details, what you order, and your birth year if you add Party Mode (18+).
  • Photos and voice recordings of the people in your game, and the stories and descriptions you send about them.
  • Payment details handled by our payment provider. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Anonymous visit statistics about this website. They use no cookies and cannot identify you.

Why we use it

  • To make your game: under our contract with you, and with the consent of every person shown in it.
  • For bookkeeping: because Danish law requires us to keep accounting records.
  • For marketing: only when you and the people shown have ticked the separate box that allows it.

Emailing yourself a list from Explore

If you ask us to email the list of things you picked on the Explore page, we use your address to send that one email and don't keep it. If you also tick the box to hear about founder spots and Christmas deadlines, we keep your address and your list until you reply "stop" or ask us to delete it.

Who else handles it

We use these services to make and deliver games: AI tools that help draw characters (OpenAI and Anthropic), Cloudflare for hosting and for storing the photos you send (Cloudflare R2), Stripe for payments, Klarna if you choose to pay in instalments, and Resend to send order emails. Photos while you build your order

On the order page, photos stay on your own device until you press Pay or Send. The quick photo check (is there one person with a clear face and their whole body in the shot, is it sharp and bright enough) runs entirely in your browser; the photo isn't sent anywhere for it. Your unfinished order is saved in your browser so you can come back to it, and it's cleared once the order is sent.

How long we keep it

  • Photos from an order that was started but never paid or sent: deleted automatically after 2 days.
  • Photos of a friend who hasn't signed the consent form within 14 days: deleted, and we tell the organiser.
  • Source photos and voice recordings: deleted 30 days after delivery.
  • Your finished game: for the hosting period of your package.
  • Accounting records: 5 years, as Danish law requires.
  • Marketing material: until consent is withdrawn.
  • An address you gave to hear about founder spots and Christmas deadlines: until you reply "stop" or ask us to delete it.

Children

We don't make games with photos of anyone under 18, and we don't accept data about children.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, object to how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. If someone shown in a game withdraws consent, we remove them from future versions and send a patched game within 14 days. Write to .

Complaints

You can complain to Datatilsynet, the Danish Data Protection Agency, at datatilsynet.dk.
