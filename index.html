    What's in your game

    A real adventure, not a greeting card

    Every game is built on the same engine as our flagship: an overworld to explore, turn-based battles with types and evolutions, and a story that ends somewhere only your group understands.

    KaiLv 12
    RicoLv 12
    Rico used Casual Nutmeg!

    Turn-based battles

    Every friend is a fighter with a type, stats and four signature moves drawn from your in-jokes. Catch the rest of the group and switch between them mid-fight.

    Your places, as the map

    The flat, the beach, the bar you always end up in. We lay the world out from your photos, so walking to the marina in the game means something.

    A story that really happened

    Lost passports, a missed flight, a stag do gone sideways. You tell us the night everyone still talks about, and it becomes the main quest.

    Meet the squad

    Real photos in, playable characters out

    These are the real people from our flagship game, Lads Trip to Albufeira, next to the characters we made from their photos. The dog counts.

    Everyone shown agreed to appear. We never use photos of children, and your source photos are deleted 30 days after delivery.
